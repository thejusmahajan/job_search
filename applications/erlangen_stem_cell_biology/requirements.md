## Overview
BMFTR-funded project focused on AI-driven integration of multi-omics data to study complex comorbidities, with a particular focus on Parkinson’s disease (PD) and inflammatory bowel disease (IBD).

## Key Tasks
- Develop and apply AI models (e.g., graph neural networks, variational autoencoders) for integrative multi-omics analysis
- Construct and analyze molecular interaction networks (transcriptomic, proteomic, metabolomic)
- Biomarker discovery and simulation-based perturbation analyses
- Collaborate with clinicians and experimental scientists
- Publish research findings and contribute to open-source software

## Requirements
- PhD in Bioinformatics, Computational Biology, Data Science, or a related field
- Strong background in machine learning and statistical modeling
- 1–3 years of postdoctoral or equivalent research experience in computational life sciences
- Track record of publications in peer-reviewed journals
- Experience with multi-omics data integration
- Proficiency in Python/R and deep learning frameworks (PyTorch, TensorFlow)
- Strong interest in translational biomedical research
