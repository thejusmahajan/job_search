## About the Lab

The Ludwig Lab develops **single-cell multi-omic approaches** to answer key questions in:
- **Stem cell biology** and hematopoiesis
- **Leukemia**
- **Mitochondrial genome biology**

### Key Technologies & Methods
- **mtscATAC-seq** (mitochondrial single-cell ATAC-seq)
- **ASAP-seq** (ATAC with Select Antigen Profiling)
- **DOGMA-seq** (chromatin accessibility + mRNA + protein profiling)
- Somatic mitochondrial mutations as natural genetic barcodes for clonal tracing

### Research Areas
1. Single-cell multi-omics method development
2. Hematopoietic stem cell heterogeneity and clonal dynamics
3. Mitochondrial genotype–phenotype links in aging and disease
