## Overview
Research project investigating cancer therapy and tumor cell biology of **renal cell carcinoma**. Focuses on bioinformatic analysis of transcriptomics data (spatial transcriptomics, single-cell datasets, spatial multiplex immunofluorescence data). 

## Key Responsibilities
- Analyze multi-omics data (single-cell and spatial transcriptomics RNA sequencing) to elucidate the tumor microenvironment in renal cell carcinoma.
- Integrate multi-omics datasets (genomics, metabolomics) of primary tumors and metastasis.
- Integrate clinical phenotypes and omics data using biostatistics and bioinformatics.
- Apply established tools and develop new computational solutions for data integration.
- Present results at international conferences and write papers for high-quality publications.

## Requirements
- PhD in bioinformatics, applied bioinformatics, or related discipline.
- Strong background in spatial transcriptomics, scRNA-seq, and multi-omics datasets (proteomics, transcriptomics, genomics).
- Strong interest in oncology and personalized medicine.
- **Strong programming skills in R and Python.**
- Solid background in cancer biology.
- Knowledge in biostatistics is beneficial.
- Excellent English communication skills (written and oral) and expertise producing scientific manuscripts.
