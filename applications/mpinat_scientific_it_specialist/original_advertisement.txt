========================================================================
Scientific IT Specialist / Research Software Engineer (f/m/x)
Job Code: 15-26

Job Offer from April 01, 2026
========================================================================

At the Max Planck Institute for Multidisciplinary Sciences, we bridge the
gap between basic research and translational, preclinical approaches.
Here, researchers from the fields of biology, chemistry, physics, and
medicine collaborate across disciplines, supported by high-performance
service facilities and a modern research infrastructure. As the largest
institute of the Max Planck Society, with around 1,000 employees from
more than 66 nations, we offer an inspiring, international working
environment with exceptional scientific breadth.

The Facility for Data Sciences and Biostatistics (Dr. Juliane Liepe)
invites applications for a

    Scientific IT Specialist / Research Software Engineer (f/m/x)

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Key tasks
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Maintain IT systems and research computing environments.
- Support and optimise data analysis workflows and pipelines.
- Install/configure scientific software.
- Assist researchers with code, HPC usage, and reproducible practices.
- Provide on-site training and contribute to best practices in research
  software.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Requirements
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Degree (MSc/PhD) in CS, bioinformatics, data science, or a related
  technical field, or equivalent professional experience.
- Strong programming skills (Python/R/Bash) and Linux.
- Experience with workflows, version control (Git), and scientific
  computing.
- Problem-solving mindset and good communication skills.
- Fluent in English.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Desired Skills
------------------------------------------------------------------------
HPC, containers (Docker/Singularity), workflow tools
(Snakemake/Nextflow), cloud, CI/CD, database systems and data storage
solutions.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
What we offer
------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Work with leading scientists.
- Work at the forefront of data-driven life science research.
- Real impact: your work directly supports scientific discoveries.
- Modern tools, HPC resources, and evolving infrastructure.
- A supportive, low-hierarchy, research-driven culture.
- Opportunities to grow technically and professionally.
- Competitive compensation and long-term career prospects.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Position details
------------------------------------------------------------------------
The position is initially limited to two years, although permanent
employment thereafter is not ruled out. Remuneration is based on the
TVoeD Bund (German federal civil service pay scale), depending on
qualifications and professional experience. In addition, public sector
social benefits apply.

We are committed to being a cosmopolitan institute that offers a diverse
and inclusive working environment with equal opportunities. We also aim
to increase the number of employees with disabilities. We welcome
applications from all backgrounds.

------------------------------------------------------------------------
Application
------------------------------------------------------------------------

background and motivation), a CV (with a publication list) and

applicable) until 8th May 2026 preferably via e-mail and as a single
