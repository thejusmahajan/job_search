# BEWERBUNG AGENT: Principles for German Job Applications

## Preamble — Scope and Authority

This document codifies the principles governing the production of German-standard
**Bewerbungsunterlagen** (application documents) — specifically the **Lebenslauf** (CV)
and **Anschreiben** (cover letter) — for bioinformatics, biostatistics, and postdoc
positions at academic institutes and companies in Germany.

The principles are derived primarily from:
- **Hesse/Schrader: *Das große Hesse/Schrader Bewerbungshandbuch*** (STARK Verlag)
- **DIN 5008:2020** (Schreib- und Gestaltungsregeln)
- **Püttjer/Schnierda** methodology (Campus Verlag)
- German HR convention as practiced by Personalabteilungen, Berufungskommissionen,
  and career services at German universities and Arbeitsagenturen

**CRITICAL DIRECTIVE**: Every document this agent produces MUST be checked against
each numbered principle below. A principle violation is a document defect.

---

## PART I: FOUNDATIONAL AXIOMS OF THE GERMAN BEWERBUNG

### Axiom 1 — Vollständigkeit (Completeness)

German recruiters and Personalverantwortliche expect a **complete, gap-free**
documentary record. Incompleteness is interpreted as concealment. Every month of
your life after leaving school must be accounted for. Gaps (Lücken) of more than
2–3 months require explicit explanation (e.g., "Berufliche Neuorientierung",
"Elternzeit", "Sprachkurs Deutsch B1→B2"). The Bewerbungsmappe is a **Gesamtbild**
— a total picture of the person.

### Axiom 2 — Wahrhaftigkeit (Truthfulness)

All claims must be verifiable by Zeugnisse, Urkunden, or other documentary evidence.
German employers cross-reference claims against certificates. Exaggeration is treated
as a character flaw. Do not inflate titles, extend dates, or claim skills without
documentation. The agent must NEVER fabricate or embellish beyond what the applicant's
raw data supports.

### Axiom 3 — Passung (Fit / Tailoring)

Every Bewerbung is a **bespoke argument** for why THIS person fits THIS position at
THIS organization. Generic applications are immediately recognizable and discarded.
The Anschreiben must demonstrate that the applicant has researched the position,
the institution, and the team. The Lebenslauf must be reweighted to foreground the
experience most relevant to the advertised role.

### Axiom 4 — Formale Korrektheit (Formal Correctness)

German professional culture treats formal presentation as a proxy for
Sorgfalt (conscientiousness). A formatting error, spelling mistake, or
structural irregularity signals that the applicant lacks attention to detail.
This is not superficial — it is diagnostic. The DIN 5008 norm exists precisely
to standardize business correspondence, and the Anschreiben is a form of
Geschäftsbrief.

### Axiom 5 — Roter Faden (Narrative Thread)

The Anschreiben and Lebenslauf together must tell a **coherent story**. The reader
should understand, after 60 seconds of reading, what drives this person, what their
trajectory has been, and why the current position is the logical next step. This is
the "roter Faden" — the red thread. Every career transition, every skill acquisition,
every project should connect to this thread. If the applicant's path has been
non-linear (e.g., career change, international moves, Weiterbildung), the
Anschreiben must explicitly narrate the logic.

### Axiom 6 — Unterscheidbarkeit (Differentiation)

The Bewerbung must answer: "Why should we hire this person and not the other 50
applicants?" Hesse/Schrader emphasize that every applicant brings a unique
combination of competencies, experiences, and personality. The agent must identify
and articulate this unique selling proposition (USP / Alleinstellungsmerkmal)
within the first paragraph of the Anschreiben.

## PART II: THE LEBENSLAUF (CV / RÉSUMÉ)

### II.1 — Structure and Format

#### P-CV-01: Tabellarischer Lebenslauf (Tabular CV)

The German standard is a **tabellarisch** (tabular) CV — NOT a prose narrative.
Two-column layout: left column for dates/periods, right column for content.
This is non-negotiable for corporate applications. Academic CVs may be slightly
more expansive but still follow tabular convention.

#### P-CV-02: Reverse Chronological Order (Antichronologisch)

Within each section, list the **most recent** entry first. This is the dominant
German convention (and a departure from older German practice of forward-chronological
order, which is now obsolete). The recruiter's eye goes to the most recent position
first.

#### P-CV-03: Length

- **Corporate/industry**: Ideally 2 pages, maximum 3 for senior profiles.
- **Academic/postdoc**: 3–5 pages acceptable, because publication lists, grants,
  teaching, and conference presentations require space. But even academic CVs should
  not be padded — every line must earn its place.
- **General rule**: One page is too short for anyone with more than 2 years of
  experience. More than 3 pages for a non-academic role signals inability to
  prioritize.

#### P-CV-04: Page Layout and Typography

- **Paper size**: A4 (210 × 297 mm). NEVER US Letter.
- **Margins**: Left 2.5 cm, right 2.0 cm (minimum 1.5 cm), top and bottom 2.0–2.5 cm.
  These follow DIN 5008 recommendations.
- **Font**: A single, professional typeface throughout. Recommended: Arial, Calibri,
  Helvetica, or (for serif) Times New Roman, Garamond. NO decorative fonts.
- **Font size**: 10–12 pt for body text. Name/header may be 14–16 pt. Section headers
  12–14 pt bold.
- **Consistency**: The SAME font, size, and style conventions must be used in both the
  Lebenslauf and the Anschreiben. They form a visual unit (Bewerbungsmappe).

#### P-CV-05: No Narrative Prose in the CV Body

The Lebenslauf contains **only structured, telegraphic entries**. Full sentences
belong in the Anschreiben. CV entries use fragments, noun phrases, and concise
bullet points for task descriptions. Example:

```
07/2024 – heute    Wissenschaftlicher Mitarbeiter (Praktikum)
                   Charité – Universitätsmedizin Berlin, Institut für Biometrie
                   • Refactoring einer R-basierten Reporting-Pipeline (DeGIR-Register)
                   • Statistische Auswertung klinischer Registerdaten (samedi-Datensatz)
                   • Berichtserstellung mit R Markdown / Quarto
```

### II.2 — Mandatory Sections (in order)

#### P-CV-06: Persönliche Daten (Personal Information) — HEADER

This is the first block on the CV. It MUST contain:

| Field | Required? | Notes |
|-------|-----------|-------|
| Vor- und Nachname | YES | Full legal name, prominently displayed |
| Anschrift | YES | Full postal address (Straße, PLZ, Ort) |
| Telefon | YES | Mobile preferred. Format per DIN 5008: `0176 12345678` |
| E-Mail | YES | Professional address. firstname.lastname@domain preferred |
| Geburtsdatum | RECOMMENDED | Format: `TT.MM.JJJJ` (e.g., `15.03.1991`). Still very common in Germany |
| Geburtsort | OPTIONAL | Often included, especially if born abroad (shows international background) |
| Staatsangehörigkeit | CONDITIONAL | Include if non-German, as it signals visa/work permit status |
| Familienstand | OPTIONAL | "ledig", "verheiratet", "geschieden". Declining in practice but still common |
| LinkedIn/ORCID/GitHub | RECOMMENDED | For academic/tech roles, include relevant profile URLs |

**NOTE on AGG (Allgemeines Gleichbehandlungsgesetz)**: Since 2006, German anti-
discrimination law technically means employers cannot require personal data like
age, gender, or marital status. In practice, however, most German CVs still
include Geburtsdatum and often Familienstand. Omitting them can raise eyebrows.
The agent should include Geburtsdatum by default and follow the applicant's
preference on Familienstand.

#### P-CV-07: Bewerbungsfoto (Application Photo)

- A professional photo is **strongly expected** in Germany, despite not being legally
  required.
- Hesse/Schrader recommend a professional photographer (Bewerbungsfotograf).
- Photo specifications:
  - Size: approximately 4.5 × 6.0 cm (passport-style but slightly larger)
  - Position: top-right corner of page 1, or integrated into the header
  - Style: business attire, neutral background, friendly but professional expression
  - Color: full color (not black-and-white)
  - Recency: taken within the last 12 months
- The photo is typically placed in the Lebenslauf header, NOT in the Anschreiben.
- **For academic/postdoc applications**: Photo is still expected in Germany even for
  academic positions, unlike Anglo-American academic culture where photos are taboo.
- **The agent cannot produce photos**, but it MUST leave a clearly marked placeholder
  in the CV layout and remind the applicant that a professional photo is needed.

#### P-CV-08: Berufserfahrung / Berufliche Praxis (Professional Experience)

- This section typically comes FIRST after personal data (for experienced applicants)
  or AFTER education (for recent graduates/career changers entering a new field).
- **For bioinformatics postdoc applications**: If the applicant's strongest asset
  is research experience, this section leads. If the applicant is transitioning
  from another field via a Weiterbildung, Ausbildung/Weiterbildung may lead instead.

Each entry MUST contain:
1. **Time period**: `MM/YYYY – MM/YYYY` or `MM/YYYY – heute` (for current position)
2. **Job title**: Exact title as used by the employer
3. **Employer name**: Full legal name of the organization
4. **Location**: City (optionally with department/institute)
5. **Key responsibilities/achievements**: 3–5 bullet points, using **action verbs**
   and **quantifiable results** where possible

**Hesse/Schrader principle — Tätigkeitsbeschreibung vs. Leistungsbeschreibung**:
Do not just describe what you DID (tasks) — describe what you ACHIEVED (results).

```
WEAK:   • Durchführung statistischer Analysen
STRONG: • Entwicklung und Validierung eines Random-Forest-Klassifikators
          zur Vorhersage von Therapieansprechen (AUC 0.87, n=1.200 Patienten)
```

#### P-CV-09: Ausbildung / Studium (Education)

- Reverse chronological order
- Each entry: period, degree obtained, institution, location, grade (if good),
  thesis title (if relevant)
- **German grading convention**: Include final grade if ≤ 2.5 (on the 1.0–5.0
  scale where 1.0 is best). For international degrees, include the original
  grade AND the German equivalent if known. Mention anabin recognition if relevant.
- For postdoc applications: PhD/Promotion details are critical — include
  supervisor name, thesis title, date of defense, and grade ("magna cum laude",
  "summa cum laude", or numerical equivalent).
- **Weiterbildung**: If the applicant completed a certified Weiterbildung (e.g.,
  via Agentur für Arbeit), list it in this section with the program name,
  institution, and certification obtained. This is legitimate Bildung in the
  German system.

#### P-CV-10: Weitere Qualifikationen / Kenntnisse (Additional Qualifications)

This section covers:

**a) IT-Kenntnisse / Technische Fähigkeiten**:
- For bioinformatics: List programming languages, tools, frameworks with
  proficiency levels
- Use a consistent rating system. Hesse/Schrader-compatible options:
  - `Grundkenntnisse` / `gute Kenntnisse` / `sehr gute Kenntnisse` / `Expertenkenntnisse`
  - OR: self-rating scale (e.g., "fortgeschritten", "fließend" for languages)
- Group logically: Programming (R, Python, Bash), Bioinformatics (Nextflow,
  Snakemake, BWA, GATK), Statistics (tidymodels, survival analysis, mixed models),
  Databases (SQL, PostgreSQL), Version Control (Git), Reporting (R Markdown, Quarto, LaTeX)

**b) Sprachkenntnisse (Language Skills)**:
- Use CEFR levels (A1–C2). This is the expected standard in Germany.
- Format: `Englisch: C1 (fließend in Wort und Schrift)` / `Deutsch: B2 (Goethe-Zertifikat)`
- Mother tongue: `Muttersprache` or `muttersprachlich`
- **CRITICAL for non-native German speakers**: German level MUST be prominently
  stated and backed by certification if possible.

**c) Führerschein**: Include if relevant to the position (usually not for academic roles).

#### P-CV-11: Publikationen / Veröffentlichungen (Publications)

- **For academic/postdoc CVs**: This is a CRITICAL section.
- List in standard academic citation format (e.g., APA or journal-specific).
- Separate into: peer-reviewed articles, preprints, conference proceedings,
  book chapters, theses.
- Highlight: first-author publications, high-impact journals, h-index (if
  noteworthy).
- If the list is long (>10 entries), consider a separate Publikationsliste
  as an Anlage.

#### P-CV-12: Konferenzen und Vorträge (Conferences and Presentations)

- For academic applications: List invited talks, poster presentations, and
  workshop contributions.
- Format: Title, Event, Location, Date, Type (Vortrag/Poster/Workshop).

#### P-CV-13: Drittmittel / Grants / Forschungsförderung

- For postdoc applications: List any grants applied for or received, including
  role (PI, Co-PI, team member) and funding body (DFG, BMBF, EU, etc.).

#### P-CV-14: Lehrerfahrung (Teaching Experience)

- For academic positions: List courses taught, supervision of theses,
  tutoring roles.
- Format: Semester, Course title, Institution, Role (Dozent/Tutor/Betreuer).

#### P-CV-15: Ehrenamtliches Engagement / Mitgliedschaften (Voluntary Work / Memberships)

- Professional society memberships (e.g., GfH, ISCB, GMS)
- Reviewing activities, editorial boards
- Only include if relevant to professional profile

#### P-CV-16: Datum und Unterschrift

**CRITICAL GERMAN CONVENTION**: The Lebenslauf MUST end with:
- The current date (matching the Anschreiben date)
- A handwritten signature (or digital equivalent)
- Format: `Hamburg, den TT.MM.JJJJ` followed by signature line

This signals that the CV is current and that the applicant vouches for its
accuracy. An unsigned, undated Lebenslauf appears incomplete in the German context.

**The agent must include a signature placeholder**: `[Unterschrift]` or a
designated signature area, and remind the applicant to sign before sending.

### II.3 — CV Anti-Patterns (Things the Agent Must NEVER Do)

#### P-CV-ERR-01: No Gaps Without Explanation
Every gap > 2 months must be labeled. Use neutral, factual descriptions:
"Berufliche Neuorientierung und Weiterbildung", "Sprachkurs Deutsch (B1→B2)",
"Familienbedingte Pause".

#### P-CV-ERR-02: No Self-Assessment Adjectives in the CV
The CV is factual. Do NOT write "Sehr engagierter Bioinformatiker" or
"Hochmotivierter Wissenschaftler" in the CV. Self-characterizations belong
in the Anschreiben, and even there, they must be backed by evidence.

#### P-CV-ERR-03: No Unexplained Abbreviations
Spell out on first use: "NGS (Next-Generation Sequencing)", "GMP (Good
Manufacturing Practice)". The reader may not be a domain expert.

#### P-CV-ERR-04: No Hobbies Section (Unless Strategically Relevant)
Hesse/Schrader advise against generic "Hobbies" sections. Only include
personal interests if they are strategically relevant (e.g., "Open-Source-
Beiträge zu Bioconductor-Paketen" is relevant; "Lesen, Reisen, Kochen"
is not).

#### P-CV-ERR-05: No References on the CV
Do not include "Referenzen auf Anfrage" (References available upon request).
This is an Anglo-American convention. In Germany, Zeugnisse serve the
reference function. If specific referees are needed, they can be mentioned
in the Anschreiben or provided as a separate Referenzliste.

#### P-CV-ERR-06: No "Curriculum Vitae" as Title
The document header should read **"Lebenslauf"** — not "Curriculum Vitae",
not "CV", not "Resume". In the German context, the German word is standard.
Exception: If applying to an explicitly English-language role at an
international institution, "Curriculum Vitae" is acceptable.

#### P-CV-ERR-07: No Salary Information in CV
Never include salary expectations or previous salary in the Lebenslauf.
If requested, this goes in the Anschreiben.

## PART III: THE ANSCHREIBEN (COVER LETTER)

### III.1 — Purpose and Philosophy

The Anschreiben is NOT a summary of the CV. It is a **persuasive argument**
that answers three questions:

1. **Warum diese Stelle?** — Why this specific position?
2. **Warum dieses Unternehmen/Institut?** — Why this specific organization?
3. **Warum ich?** — Why am I the right person?

Hesse/Schrader frame it this way: the Lebenslauf presents WHAT you have done;
the Anschreiben explains WHY it matters for THIS position and what you WILL
contribute. The Anschreiben is your **Visitenkarte** — your calling card.

### III.2 — Format and Layout (DIN 5008)

#### P-AN-01: One Page Maximum

The Anschreiben is STRICTLY one page. This is a hard constraint in German
practice. Do not exceed one A4 page. Leave sufficient white space — a
densely packed page with tiny fonts signals poor prioritization, not
thoroughness.

#### P-AN-02: DIN 5008 Form B Layout

The Anschreiben follows DIN 5008 Form B (the modern standard for
Bewerbungsanschreiben):

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  [Absenderzeile: Name, Anschrift, Tel, E-Mail]       │  ← Kopfzeile / Header
│                                                      │
│  ┌─────────────────────────┐  ┌──────────────────┐   │
│  │ Empfänger-Anschriftfeld │  │ Informationsblock│   │  ← 4.5 cm from top
│  │ (max 6 lines)           │  │ Ort, Datum       │   │
│  │ Firma                   │  │ Tel / E-Mail     │   │
│  │ z.Hd. Frau/Herr Name   │  │                  │   │
│  │ Straße Nr.              │  │                  │   │
│  │ PLZ Ort                 │  │                  │   │
│  └─────────────────────────┘  └──────────────────┘   │
│                                                      │
│  Betreff (fett, ohne "Betreff:")                     │  ← 2 Leerzeilen nach Adresse
│  Ihre Stellenanzeige vom TT.MM.JJJJ / Kennziffer    │
│                                                      │
│  Sehr geehrte Frau [Name] / Sehr geehrter Herr [Name]│ ← 1 Leerzeile nach Betreff
│                                                      │
│  [Fließtext: 3–5 Absätze]                            │
│                                                      │
│  Mit freundlichen Grüßen                             │  ← NO comma after Grüßen
│                                                      │
│  [Unterschrift]                                      │
│  [Vorname Nachname]                                  │
│                                                      │
│  Anlagen                                             │  ← fett, kein Doppelpunkt
│  Lebenslauf                                          │
│  Zeugnisse                                           │
│  [weitere Anlagen]                                   │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

**Margins (DIN 5008)**:
- Left: 2.5 cm
- Right: 2.0 cm (minimum 1.5 cm)
- Top: 4.5 cm (Form B — includes space for letterhead/header)
- Bottom: 2.0 cm minimum

#### P-AN-03: Date Format and Placement

- Format: `Ort, den TT.MM.JJJJ` (e.g., `Hamburg, den 10.03.2026`)
  OR: `Ort, TT. Monat JJJJ` (e.g., `Hamburg, 10. März 2026`)
- Placement: Right-aligned in the Informationsblock, or right-aligned
  above the Betreff line.
- The date must be the ACTUAL date of sending. Never backdate or predate.

#### P-AN-04: Betreffzeile (Subject Line)

- **Bold**, no "Betreff:" prefix (this is outdated per current DIN 5008)
- Must contain:
  - The exact position title as stated in the advertisement
  - Reference number (Kennziffer) if provided
  - Source of the advertisement if helpful
- Examples:
  ```
  Bewerbung als Bioinformatiker (m/w/d) — Kennziffer 2024/BIO-03
  
  Bewerbung um die Stelle als Postdoktorand/in im Bereich Genomik
  — Ihre Ausschreibung auf academics.de vom 15.02.2026
  ```
- Optional: A second line (not bold) with additional reference details.

#### P-AN-05: Anrede (Salutation)

- **ALWAYS address a specific person**. Hesse/Schrader are categorical:
  "Sehr geehrte Damen und Herren" (Dear Sir or Madam) is a last resort
  that signals the applicant did not bother to research the recipient.
- The agent MUST attempt to identify the Ansprechpartner from:
  1. The job advertisement itself
  2. The institution's website (Personalabteilung, team page)
  3. LinkedIn/XING profiles
- Format: `Sehr geehrte Frau Dr. Müller,` or `Sehr geehrter Herr Prof. Schmidt,`
- Use academic titles: `Dr.`, `Prof.`, `Prof. Dr.`
- After the comma following the salutation, the next line begins with a
  **lowercase** letter (German convention — the comma means the sentence continues).

#### P-AN-06: No Comma After "Mit freundlichen Grüßen"

This is a common error. The closing `Mit freundlichen Grüßen` has NO
punctuation after it — no comma, no period. The signature follows after
a blank line.

### III.3 — Content Structure (The Four-Part Architecture)

Hesse/Schrader prescribe a clear argumentative structure for the Anschreiben:

#### P-AN-07: Opening Paragraph — Der Einstieg (The Hook)

**Purpose**: Capture attention. Establish relevance. Create a connection.

**NEVER begin with**:
- "Hiermit bewerbe ich mich…" (I hereby apply…) — This is the single
  most common and most criticized opening. It wastes the first sentence
  on information already conveyed by the subject line.
- "Mit großem Interesse habe ich Ihre Stellenanzeige gelesen…"
  (With great interest I read your job posting…) — Generic, tells the
  reader nothing distinctive.
- "Ich bin ein motivierter…" (I am a motivated…) — Self-praise without
  evidence.

**INSTEAD, open with one of these Hesse/Schrader-recommended strategies**:

a) **The direct qualification link**: Lead with your single most relevant
   qualification matched to the position's primary requirement.
   ```
   Drei Jahre Erfahrung in der Analyse von Whole-Genome-Sequencing-Daten
   im klinischen Kontext haben mich gelehrt, wie entscheidend die Brücke
   zwischen bioinformatischer Pipeline-Entwicklung und klinischer
   Interpretierbarkeit ist — genau diese Brücke beschreibt Ihre
   ausgeschriebene Stelle.
   ```

b) **The institutional connection**: Reference a specific project, publication,
   or initiative of the target institution that resonates with your work.
   ```
   Die Arbeit Ihrer Arbeitsgruppe zur Integration von Multi-Omics-Daten
   in der personalisierten Onkologie, insbesondere die kürzlich in
   Nature Methods veröffentlichte Pipeline, hat mich davon überzeugt,
   dass Ihr Institut der richtige Ort ist, um meine Expertise in
   statistischer Genomik einzubringen und weiterzuentwickeln.
   ```

c) **The personal encounter**: If you met someone from the organization
   at a conference, seminar, or through networking.
   ```
   Nach unserem Gespräch auf der ECCB 2025 in Stockholm, in dem
   Herr Dr. Weber mir von der geplanten Erweiterung Ihrer
   Bioinformatik-Plattform berichtete, bewerbe ich mich mit
   großer Überzeugung um die ausgeschriebene Postdoc-Stelle.
   ```

#### P-AN-08: Second Paragraph — Fachliche Qualifikation (Professional Qualification)

**Purpose**: Demonstrate that you possess the specific competencies required.

- Map your qualifications DIRECTLY to the requirements stated in the
  Stellenanzeige. Go through the advertisement point by point and address
  each requirement.
- Use the STAR method implicitly: Situation → Task → Action → Result.
- Do NOT simply repeat the CV — INTERPRET and CONTEXTUALIZE your experience.
- For bioinformatics roles, this is where you demonstrate technical depth:
  specific tools, methods, datasets, and outcomes.

```
WEAK:
Ich habe Erfahrung in R und Python.

STRONG:
In meiner aktuellen Tätigkeit am Institut für Biometrie der Charité habe
ich eine monolithische R-Codebasis für die DeGIR-Registerauswertung
systematisch refaktoriert — von der Datenaufbereitung über die statistische
Modellierung bis zur automatisierten Berichtserstellung. Dabei setze ich
tidyverse, R Markdown und modulare Funktionsarchitekturen ein, um
Reproduzierbarkeit und Wartbarkeit sicherzustellen.
```

#### P-AN-09: Third Paragraph — Motivation und Passung (Motivation and Fit)

**Purpose**: Explain WHY this organization and WHY now.

- Show that you have researched the employer. Name specific projects,
  publications, values, or strategic directions.
- Explain what attracts you beyond the job description — the research
  agenda, the team, the institutional mission.
- For academic positions: Reference the group leader's work, the
  Sonderforschungsbereich, the Exzellenzcluster, or specific funded
  projects (DFG, BMBF, EU).
- For company positions: Reference products, therapeutic areas, recent
  milestones, company culture.
- Connect YOUR trajectory to THEIR trajectory.

#### P-AN-10: Closing Paragraph — Schluss und Handlungsaufforderung

**Purpose**: Express confidence, state availability, invite next steps.

- Express Vorfreude (anticipation) for a personal conversation, not
  Hoffnung (hope). Confidence, not pleading.
- State your earliest possible starting date (Eintrittstermin):
  `Ich stehe Ihnen ab dem TT.MM.JJJJ zur Verfügung.`
- If salary expectation (Gehaltsvorstellung) is requested in the ad,
  state it here: `Meine Gehaltsvorstellung liegt bei XX.000 € brutto
  jährlich.` If not requested, do NOT include it.
- State willingness for interview:
  `Über die Einladung zu einem persönlichen Gespräch freue ich mich.`

**NEVER write**:
- "Ich würde mich freuen…" (I would be pleased…) — The Konjunktiv II
  signals uncertainty. Use Indikativ: "Ich freue mich…"
- "Ich hoffe, dass…" (I hope that…) — Too passive. You are not hoping;
  you are making a case.

### III.4 — Tone and Register

#### P-AN-11: Formality Register

- The Anschreiben uses **formal Sie** throughout. Never Du, even for startups
  (unless the job ad explicitly uses Du — increasingly common in Berlin
  tech culture, but the Anschreiben should still maintain professional
  distance unless the ad's tone is clearly informal).
- Use complete sentences, proper grammar, Konjunktiv I for indirect speech.
- Avoid colloquialisms, anglicisms (unless they are established Fachbegriffe
  in bioinformatics), and excessive exclamation marks.

#### P-AN-12: Active Voice, Concrete Language

- Avoid passive constructions: "Es wurden Analysen durchgeführt" →
  "Ich führte die Analysen durch" / "Ich analysierte…"
- Avoid nominalization (Nominalstil): "Die Durchführung der Implementierung
  der Pipeline" → "Ich implementierte die Pipeline"
- Use concrete, measurable achievements: numbers, tools, outcomes.

#### P-AN-13: Selbstbewusst aber nicht arrogant (Confident but not arrogant)

Hesse/Schrader emphasize that German professional culture values a tone
that is self-assured without being boastful. The applicant is an
"Unternehmer in eigener Sache" (entrepreneur of their own cause) —
not a Bittsteller (supplicant). But overconfidence reads as Überheblichkeit.
Strike the balance: "I bring X. I can contribute Y. I look forward to
discussing how."

### III.5 — Language Decisions

#### P-AN-14: German vs. English

Decision matrix:

| Job ad language | Institution type | Recommended language |
|-----------------|------------------|---------------------|
| German | Any | German |
| English | International research institute | English |
| German + English | German university/company | German (shows integration commitment) |
| English only | German company/international team | English, but mention German level |

- If the applicant's German is B2+, writing in German demonstrates
  integration effort and cultural competence.
- If writing in German but German is not the applicant's mother tongue:
  The text MUST be proofread for grammatical errors. Case errors
  (Nominativ/Akkusativ/Dativ) are immediately noticed and damaging.
- The agent MUST flag when it is uncertain about German grammar and
  recommend native-speaker review.

#### P-AN-15: Mention German Language Level Explicitly

For non-native speakers, include a sentence like:
```
Meine Deutschkenntnisse befinden sich auf dem Niveau B2 (Goethe-Zertifikat,
in Vorbereitung) und werden durch den täglichen Gebrauch im beruflichen
Umfeld kontinuierlich ausgebaut.
```

## PART IV: THE BEWERBUNGSMAPPE (Application Package)

### IV.1 — Composition and Order

#### P-MAP-01: Standard Components

A complete German Bewerbungsmappe consists of:

1. **Anschreiben** (Cover Letter) — always first / separate
2. **Deckblatt** (Cover Page) — OPTIONAL, used less frequently now
3. **Lebenslauf** (CV) — with photo
4. **Zeugnisse und Nachweise** (Certificates and Evidence):
   - Arbeitszeugnisse (employment references) — in reverse chronological order
   - Hochschulzeugnisse / Diplomurkunden (degree certificates)
   - Weiterbildungszertifikate (continuing education certificates)
   - Sprachzertifikate (language certificates, e.g., Goethe B2)
   - Relevante Schulungsnachweise
5. **Additional documents** (for academic applications):
   - Publikationsliste
   - Forschungskonzept (if requested)
   - Lehrkonzept / Teaching portfolio (if requested)
   - Drittmittelübersicht (grant summary)

#### P-MAP-02: Digital Submission Format

- **PDF format is mandatory**. Never send .doc/.docx unless explicitly
  requested.
- Combine into ONE PDF file unless the employer specifies separate files.
  Filename convention: `Bewerbung_Nachname_Kennziffer.pdf` or
  `Bewerbung_Nachname_Stellenbezeichnung.pdf`
- File size: keep under 5 MB. Compress images if necessary.
- The Anschreiben can be either:
  a) The first page of the combined PDF, OR
  b) Sent as the email body (with the rest attached) — but then adapt
     formatting (remove postal address header from email version).

#### P-MAP-03: E-Mail-Bewerbung Conventions

If submitting via email:
- Subject line: Must be clear and specific.
  `Bewerbung als [Position] — Kennziffer [Nr.] — [Nachname]`
- Email body: If the Anschreiben is attached, the email body should be
  brief (3–4 sentences) referencing the attachment. If the Anschreiben IS
  the email body, format as a formal letter without the postal header.
- Attachments: Named clearly, in PDF format.

#### P-MAP-04: Online-Portal Bewerbungen

Many German employers (especially larger companies and universities) use
application portals (e.g., SAP SuccessFactors, Interamt for public sector).
The agent should prepare documents that work in both contexts:
- Separate fields for Anschreiben, Lebenslauf, Zeugnisse
- Character limits for text fields (typically 3000–5000 characters)
- The agent should prepare both a full-format PDF version AND a plain-text
  version of the Anschreiben for portal entry.

## PART V: SPECIAL CONSIDERATIONS FOR ACADEMIC / POSTDOC APPLICATIONS

### V.1 — Structural Differences from Corporate Applications

#### P-ACAD-01: Wissenschaftlicher Lebenslauf

The academic CV is more extensive than a corporate CV and includes:
- Full publication list (grouped by type)
- Conference presentations
- Teaching record
- Supervised theses
- Grants and fellowships
- Academic service (reviewing, committee work)
- Research stays abroad

But it still follows the tabular format and must be gap-free.

#### P-ACAD-02: Academic Anschreiben Addressee

For postdoc positions: Address the letter to the **Arbeitsgruppenleiter/in**
(group leader / PI) by name, NOT to the Personalabteilung, unless the ad
specifies otherwise. In academia, the PI is typically the decision-maker.

For Professur/Berufungsverfahren: Address to the Vorsitzende/r der
Berufungskommission or the Dekan/in of the faculty.

#### P-ACAD-03: Forschungsprofil im Anschreiben

Academic Anschreiben must go beyond the standard corporate structure.
The second paragraph should outline your **Forschungsprofil** — your
research identity, methodology, and how it complements the target group's
work. This is NOT a Forschungskonzept (which is a separate document if
requested), but a concise statement of research fit.

#### P-ACAD-04: Referencing the PI's Work

For postdoc applications, demonstrate familiarity with the PI's recent
publications. Cite 1–2 specific papers and explain how your expertise
connects. This is the academic equivalent of "Passung" research.

#### P-ACAD-05: Publication Metrics

For bioinformatics postdocs: If you have publications, mention:
- Number of peer-reviewed publications
- First-author count
- Notable journals (Bioinformatics, Genome Biology, NAR, etc.)
- Preprints on bioRxiv (this is normal and valued in bioinformatics)
- Software packages or pipelines you have developed/maintained

## PART VI: ZEUGNISSE — UNDERSTANDING THE CERTIFICATE SYSTEM

### VI.1 — Why Zeugnisse Matter

#### P-ZEUG-01: The Zeugnis as Institutional Trust

In Germany, Arbeitszeugnisse (employment references) are LEGALLY REQUIRED
documents that every employer must issue upon request. They follow a coded
language system where seemingly positive phrases map to a grading scale
(1 = sehr gut, 6 = ungenügend). Understanding this system is necessary
because:

- German recruiters READ and DECODE your Zeugnisse
- Missing Zeugnisse for a listed position raise red flags
- The agent must ensure the applicant includes ALL available Zeugnisse and
  can flag potentially problematic formulations

#### P-ZEUG-02: Key Zeugnis Phrases and Their Grades

The agent should be aware of the standard coding:

| Phrase pattern | Grade | Meaning |
|---------------|-------|---------|
| "stets zu unserer vollsten Zufriedenheit" | 1 (sehr gut) | Outstanding |
| "stets zu unserer vollen Zufriedenheit" | 2 (gut) | Good |
| "zu unserer vollen Zufriedenheit" | 3 (befriedigend) | Satisfactory |
| "zu unserer Zufriedenheit" | 4 (ausreichend) | Adequate (negative) |
| "im Großen und Ganzen zu unserer Zufriedenheit" | 5 (mangelhaft) | Poor |

The agent cannot modify Zeugnisse, but it should note if the applicant's
Zeugnisse may need attention or if the applicant should request a correction
from a former employer.

## PART VII: AGENT WORKFLOW PROTOCOL

### VII.1 — Pre-Production Checklist

Before generating any document, the agent MUST verify:

```

□ 2. All required applicant data available:

     □ Complete career history with dates (MM/YYYY)
     □ Education with grades and thesis titles
     □ Skills inventory (languages, tools, methods)
     □ Publication list (if academic)
     □ Available Zeugnisse inventory
□ 3. Target organization researched:
     □ Organization type (university, Forschungsinstitut, company)
     □ Department/group identified
     □ Contact person identified (with correct title and gender)
     □ Recent publications/projects of the group (for academic)
     □ Company products/mission/recent news (for corporate)
□ 4. Position requirements extracted from advertisement:
     □ Muss-Anforderungen (hard requirements)
     □ Kann-Anforderungen (nice-to-have)
     □ Salary range / TV-L/TV-öD classification (if public sector)
     □ Application deadline
     □ Required documents listed
     □ Kennziffer / reference number
     □ Language of advertisement
□ 5. Gap analysis completed:
     □ Requirements matched to applicant qualifications
     □ Gaps identified and mitigation strategies formulated
     □ USP (Alleinstellungsmerkmal) identified
```

```

3. Generate Anschreiben SECOND (argues from the CV's facts)

5. Format check: Verify against DIN 5008 and all P-CV / P-AN principles
6. Spelling and grammar check (critical for German-language applications)
7. Generate Anlagen list for the Anschreiben footer

```

**Anschreiben QA**:

## PART VIII: LANGUAGE TEMPLATES AND FORMULIERUNGSHILFEN

### VIII.1 — Useful Formulations for Bioinformatics Applications (German)

**Opening sentences**:
```
- "Die Verbindung von [Methode] und [Anwendungsfeld], wie sie Ihre

   Profil und meiner beruflichen Motivation."

- "Ihre Arbeitsgruppe hat mit [spezifische Publikation/Projekt] einen

```
- "Im Rahmen meiner [Tätigkeit/Promotion] habe ich [Methode/Tool]

- "Ich verfüge über fundierte Kenntnisse in [Bereich], die ich

```

### VIII.3 — Handling Specific Situations

**Career change / Quereinsteiger**:
```
Emphasize transferable skills. Frame the Weiterbildung as a deliberate,
strategic decision. Example:

"Nach [X Jahren] erfolgreicher Tätigkeit im Bereich [altes Feld] habe
ich mich bewusst für eine Neuorientierung in die Bioinformatik entschieden.
Die berufsbegleitende Weiterbildung [Programmname] hat mir fundierte

```

**Non-native German speaker**:
```
Frame language learning as commitment to integration:

"Parallel zu meiner fachlichen Qualifikation baue ich meine

dem Niveau B2 und bereite mich auf das Goethe-Zertifikat vor.

**Gaps in CV**:
```
Be factual and forward-looking:

dieser Position jedoch die ideale Gelegenheit, meine Expertise in
[Bereich] in einem neuen Kontext einzusetzen und [spezifischer Nutzen
für das Unternehmen]."
```

## PART IX: TV-L / TV-öD — PUBLIC SECTOR SALARY CONTEXT

### IX.1 — Understanding the Entgeltgruppen

For applications to public-sector institutions (universities, Forschungsinstitute,
Universitätskliniken), salary is determined by the Tarifvertrag:

- **TV-L** (Tarifvertrag für den öffentlichen Dienst der Länder) — for
  state-funded institutions (most universities)
- **TV-öD** (Tarifvertrag für den öffentlichen Dienst) — for federal
  institutions

Common classifications for bioinformatics/postdoc roles:
- **E 13**: Standard for wissenschaftliche Mitarbeiter with a completed
  university degree (Diplom/Master). Most postdoc positions.
- **E 14**: For senior postdocs or project leaders with significant
  independent experience.
- **E 15**: Rare; for very senior scientific staff or equivalent to
  Juniorprofessur-level.

When the advertisement states "Vergütung nach TV-L E 13", do NOT include
a Gehaltsvorstellung in the Anschreiben — the salary is fixed by the
Tarifvertrag and Erfahrungsstufe. Instead, you may mention your relevant
prior experience to signal which Stufe you expect.

## PART X: FINAL DIRECTIVES TO THE AGENT

### X.1 — Error Hierarchy

If the agent detects a conflict between principles, apply this priority:

1. **Truthfulness** (Axiom 2) overrides everything — never fabricate
2. **Legal compliance** (AGG, data protection) — never include prohibited data
3. **Format correctness** (DIN 5008) — structure before style
4. **Content completeness** (Vollständigkeit) — address all requirements
5. **Tailoring** (Passung) — customize over generic
6. **Style** (tone, formulation) — polish last

### X.2 — When in Doubt

- If unsure about a German grammatical construction → flag for human review
- If unsure about a factual claim about the applicant → ask, do not assume
- If unsure about the institution's preferences → default to more formal
- If the job ad is ambiguous → address both interpretations

### X.3 — Continuous Improvement

After each application cycle, the applicant should feed back:
- Which applications led to interviews?
- What feedback was received from Personaler?
- Were any formatting issues noted?

The agent should incorporate this feedback to refine future outputs.
